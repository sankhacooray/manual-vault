How it works

Scan once. Find it whenever
something breaks.

No typing model numbers, no hunting a manufacturer's site. The QR code on the paper already points at the PDF.

Scan the QR PDF opens Saved to your Drive Claude files it Search by problem

01Scan the QR code

The camera opens with autofocus, tap to focus and zoom. The PDF opens the moment the code is read.

02It lands in your Drive

Saved to a Manual Vault folder in your own Google Drive. Scan the same manual twice and it's spotted.

03Claude files it

Claude reads each PDF and gives it a title, brand, model, category and tags, then renames the file to match.

Privacy

Your Drive. Your key.
Nothing in between.

Sign in with any Google account. Your vault is a folder in your own Drive, and the app asks for as little as it can.

Only its own files

Uses Google's narrow drive.file permission. The app can't open, change or delete anything else in your Drive.

Your own Claude key

Kept in your Google user properties for this app only. Skip it and manuals still save, just without indexing.

No server of ours

It runs as you inside Google Apps Script, so nothing passes through us. The code is open source on GitHub.
